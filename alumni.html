A proud legacy

Prominent graduates

Leaders, public servants and scholars from Government College Bida continue to make a lasting difference.

National leadership

General Ibrahim Badamasi Babangida GCFR

Former Nigerian Military Head of State (1985–1993), Babangida attended Government College Bida from 1957 to 1962 and later served as Chief of Army Staff before becoming head of state.

Public service

General Abdulsalami Abubakar GCFR

A Government College Bida alumnus, Abubakar served as Nigeria’s Head of State from 1998 to 1999 and oversaw the transition to civilian government.

Former BOSA President

Col. Sani Bello (Rtd.)

Bello attended Government College Bida from 1957 to 1962, served as military governor of old Kano State, and is recognised as a former BOSA National President.
