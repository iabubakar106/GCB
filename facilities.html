School Facilities

Spaces and resources that support life and learning at GCB.

Our campus

Facilities for every part of school life

From study and digital learning to health services and practical science, these facilities support students throughout their school experience.

Student support

Information & health

Computer Lab

Equipped with desktop computers and networking peripherals, the lab supports computer studies, e-learning and preparation for computer-based tests such as JAMB and BECE.

IBB Library

A quiet study environment with textbooks, reference materials and literature. The library supports independent study and literacy development across grade levels.

School Clinic

Staffed by trained medical personnel, the clinic provides first aid and primary health services, manages minor ailments and responds to emergencies in the boarding community.

Practical learning

Science laboratories

Biology Lab

Biological specimens, microscopes and anatomical charts support the study of living organisms, plant physiology and the dissection exercises required for WASSCE.

Chemistry Lab

Workbenches with sinks and drainage, essential reagents, glassware and safety equipment support chemical analysis, titrations and qualitative experiments.

Physics Lab

Apparatus for mechanics, optics, electricity and magnetism allows students to explore physical laws through experiments with circuits, lenses and pendulums.
