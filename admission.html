Admission to GCB

Admission procedures and essential information for families.

Getting started

Your admission guide

Admission to Government College Bida, a state-owned secondary school in Niger State, follows the standardized process managed by the Niger State Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education Board. Review the requirements for each entry level below.

Entry level 01

Junior Secondary School

JSS 1 admission

Admission at this level is for pupils completing their primary education.

  • Age: Candidates are typically at least 12 years old by September 1 of the enrolment year.
  • Application: Request a free admission form from Government College Bida. The school submits completed forms to the State Ministry of Education.
  • Placement: Successful candidates are placed into Government College Bida. There is usually no admission for JSS 2 or JSS 3.
Entry level 02

Senior Secondary School

SS 1 admission

Admission into the senior section is for students transitioning from Junior Secondary School.

  • BECE: Pass the Basic Education Certificate Examination conducted by NECO or the State Ministry of Education.
  • Application: Request a free admission form from Government College Bida. The school submits completed forms to the State Ministry of Education.
  • Placement list: The Ministry releases the official admission list, often through the Niger State Ministry of Education portal.

Documents for clearance

  1. Original admission letter printed from the Ministry’s portal.
  2. BECE statement of result (SS 1 only).
  3. Birth certificate or official declaration of age.
  4. Certificate of indigene from the student’s Local Government Area.
  5. Medical certificate of fitness from a government-approved hospital.
Please note

Key rules and levies

What families should know

  • Fees: Tuition is generally covered by the state; students are required to pay a PTA levy to the school.
  • Uniforms: Parents provide uniforms and sportswear based on samples displayed at the school.
  • Prohibited items: Electronics, mobile phones (GSM) and dangerous weapons are strictly prohibited on school premises.

For application forms and current admission guidance, contact Government College Bida.
